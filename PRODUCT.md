# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Personal use — a single student studying for the CCNA certification. Used at a desk, typically in focused study sessions, to track lab completion, take quizzes, and review analytics on study progress.

## Product Purpose
A local-first CCNA study tracker that organizes Packet Tracer labs, quiz practice, and progress analytics in one place. Success means the user can see exactly where they stand across all CCNA topics and what to focus on next.

## Brand Personality
Focused, clean, functional. The interface stays out of the way and lets the data speak. No unnecessary decoration; every element earns its screen space.

## Anti-references
- Gamified study apps with badges, streaks, and confetti
- Dense enterprise dashboards with dozens of widgets
- Generic SaaS landing pages

## Design Principles
1. **Progress at a glance** — the current state of study should be obvious within seconds of opening the app.
2. **Zero friction** — launching a lab or starting a quiz should take one click, not three.
3. **Honest data** — show real progress, including weak areas, without sugar-coating.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA. Support for light and dark themes (already implemented). Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
